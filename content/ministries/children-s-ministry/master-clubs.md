At Faith Baptist Church we recognize that our children are the next generation of spiritual leaders for the church and we have provided every opportunity for them to be a part of the ministry now as well as learning how to display and defend their beliefs.

On Wednesday Evenings we have a program called Master Clubs which provides the children with a structured and fun way to learn about the different doctrines or teachings of the Bible, as well as provide them provide them opportunities to serve the Master. As the children are able to work for various badges as they are learning various Bible verses and how they apply to their daily lives. This approach provides us with a way to encourage the children to not only memorize what the Bible has to say, but also puts those verses into a context that the children will be able to use during their lives.

Please come by to see the impact that God is having in Raymore and our neighboring communities as we seek to honor and glorify Him with our lives.
