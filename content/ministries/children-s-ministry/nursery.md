# Nursery (0-3 years)

#### Mat 19:14 But Jesus said, Suffer little children, and forbid them not, to come unto me: for of such is the kingdom of heaven.

For each of our worship services, your children will enjoy a nursery that is clean, secure, and staffed with friendly and well-qualified volunteers. Our teachers and workers have not only approved background checks, but have experience and training in working in children's ministries.

Our goal is to put your mind at ease when you enroll your child. Feel free to inform the nursery workers of any specific directions or important instructions regarding your child’s care. We strive to take care of every child as if that child was our own.
