Regardless of your age or background, we have a place for every lady at Faith Baptist Church! Our church ladies have many opportunities throughout the year to learn from God’s Word, encourage one another, use individual talents to serve God, and just have lots of fun! The Titus 2 Sisters is a ladies Bible study that meets regularly from the fall through spring and is designed for ladies of any age. Other events that help ladies connect and form meaningful friendships include an annual ladies banquet, special dinners, a ladies retreat, and more! You can get to know others by getting involved! It doesn’t take much searching to find a place to use your talents. Some possibilities include the music ministry, infant and toddler nursery, teaching or assisting in children’s classes, and helping with meals for those in need throughout the year. We believe every lady should be learning and growing through regular study of God’s Word and consistently using her talents for the Lord in the home and the local church.

![](/images/Titus2Sisters.pjg.jpg)

In Titus 2:3-5 we see God’s command for aged (spiritually mature) women to be teaching other women. Each year from fall through the spring, the Titus 2 Sisters Bible study is underway two Thursday nights a month. It is attended by ladies of all ages; and for moms with young children, a nursery is provided. Our time each evening includes refreshments, an in depth study of God’s Word, and ends with a time of prayer. Regardless of what today’s culture or society values, our main goal is to seek the answers from God’s Word and apply His wisdom to our lives.
