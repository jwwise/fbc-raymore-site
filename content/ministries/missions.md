## MISSIONS CONFERENCE

Faith Baptist has the honor of supporting twenty missionaries around the world. Faith hosts a mission conference each year and looks for opportunities to expand our heart for world-wide missions. Faith had the privilege to put together 18,000 scriptures in conjunction with Midwest Seedline and have had the opportunity to host many visiting missionary families. We look forward to the possibility of supporting more missionaries in the future.

## MISSIONARIES

![](/images/missions_world.png)

The task of taking the Good News of Jesus to the distant regions of earth is the duty of every church and believer. Our worldwide support of missionaries and Gospel works is how Lakeside is fulfilling its duty. With over one works scattered in all directions, truly the “sun never sets” on the work of Faith. Our support and communication is directly with the missionaries. We often have them home and present in our services to present new ministries and to report on the blessings.

## MISSION TRIPS

Groups from Faith have gone on trips to different parts of the world. These trips are wonderful experiences for our people to spread the gospel abroad. We look forward to future opportunities to serve the Lord on many more mission trips.

## LOCAL OUTREACH

It can be said that every Christian is called to be a missionary. While some are called to distant regions all over the globe, others are called to minister at home. Members of Faith take time regularly to reach those in our local communities through door-to-door outreach, "So Loved Saturdays". We distribute gospel literature as well as information regarding upcoming events. God has blessed, and we have witnessed God working in the hearts of many as the flock of Faith grows!

![](/images/missions2.jpg)

Please join with us in prayer for our missionaries that God will protect them and flourish their work according to His will.
