Meet the Pastor

Michael graduated from Pensacola Christian College with a degree in youth ministries. It was there that he met his wife Candace, who grew up in West Columbia, South Carolina. Michael and she were married on July 31, 1999. For the next five years the couple served in two different churches as the youth pastor and a Christian school teacher. In January 2005, the Wessbergs joined the Tozour Evangelistic Team to start a children’s ministry and assist in music with Rich and Angela. In February of 2016 God led the Wessbergs to Faith Baptist Church where he serves as senior pastor. The Wessbergs have seven children: Zachariah, Andrew, Chloe, Evy, Lilly, Isaac and Asher.

Meet the Music Director

Jim and Priscilla Stephens enjoy the ministry at Faith. The church ministry has been a blessing to us.
I am a native South Carolinian and grew up in a pastor's home. Priscilla grew up in Bolivia, South America. Her parents were missionaries. We met while students at Tennessee Temple University and were married in 1972. We have three children, all graduates of Christian colleges and are serving the Lord. We have "no greater joy than to hear that our children walk in truth". And, we enjoy our 14 grandchildren.
I had the distinct privilege of serving our country in the US Army from 1967-1970 as a paratrooper with the 101st Airborne Division in Vietnam and the 82nd Airborne Division stateside. I am grateful for our great country.
We were involved in Christian Education for 30 plus years and also have served on the church staff of churches in Tennessee, North Carolina, and South Carolina. We were also the directors for a camp for "at-risk-teens" for 5 years. During that time we were able to reach many teens for Christ.
We moved to Raymore to help with the homeschooling for our grandchildren.
Our mission continues to be Jude 22..."making a difference" in the lives of people.
