Children's Ministry

The children's ministry of any church is vital to the health of the church not only immediately, but for the long term. Our prayer is for the Lord of the Harvest to use our children's ministry to raise up laborers that the He can use for the glory of His name. We are praying for pastors, missionaries, Sunday school teachers, deacons, nursery workers, etc. to come from our children's ministry to be leaders in the church of the next generation. We feel this can be accomplished through prayer and the teaching of God's Word to our children.


Our various children's ministries are set up not only to reach our children with the truth of the gospel, but to train our children to defend and live for the truth of the gospel. Our desire is to come alongside parents, not as the primary source, but an important assisting source, helping them in training their children in the way they should go from the truths of the Bible.

Our teachers and workers have not only approved background checks, but have experience and training in working in children's ministries.  Your child will be loved and cared for by these servants of God. Our prayer at Faith Baptist Church is that your child will be blessed and grow in a greater knowledge of God and His Word through our children's ministries.
