Pastor's Letter

Our church family would like to welcome you to Faith Baptist Church. We trust that you would find Faith to be a warm and friendly, exciting and growing, Bible believing, Bible preaching family-oriented church.

The mission of Faith Baptist Church is to impact our community and proclaim Jesus Christ to those who may not know Him, instruct them as they grow in the Lord, include them in the fellowship of our church, involve them in opportunities to serve, using their unique gifts and abilities, and inspire them to be all they can be for the Lord.

If you do not have a church home, or are looking for a new one, we would be honored to have you consider coming to worship with us. We believe Faith Baptist Church to be a great place for you and your family!

To be a reflection of Him,

Pastor Wessberg
